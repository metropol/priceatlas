# Privacy Policy

**PriceAtlas**
Published by Atelier Artisanal Digital, the operating name of Ugo Di Profio, sole proprietor, Montréal, Quebec, Canada.

Last updated: 13 August 2026

---

## Summary

PriceAtlas is a command-line tool that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server component, no user accounts, and no telemetry. It does not send your pricing data, your app data, or your usage of the tool to us or to anyone else.

The only personal information we hold about you is what is necessary to sell you a licence and provide support.

---

## 1. Information the software collects

**None.**

PriceAtlas runs locally on your machine. Specifically:

- It does not require an account or a sign-in.
- It does not transmit your prices, your app identifiers, your reports, or your configuration anywhere.
- It contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
- It does not collect or transmit your Apple Developer credentials. If you use the optional App Store Connect features, authentication is handled by asccli (https://asccli.sh), a separate third-party tool that you install and configure yourself. PriceAtlas never receives, stores, or transmits those credentials.

If PriceAtlas retrieves reference data (such as published purchasing-power or exchange-rate figures) over the network, those requests are made to public data sources and contain no information about you, your apps, or your prices.

---

## 2. Information we collect when you buy a licence

Payments are processed by **Creem** (Creem is the merchant of record and the seller of record for your purchase). Creem collects your payment details directly. **We never see or hold your full payment card details.**

From Creem, we receive:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your country (for tax purposes)
- The amount, date, and currency of your purchase

We use this information to:

- Deliver your licence and access instructions
- Provide customer support
- Process refunds
- Meet our record-keeping and tax obligations

Creem's own handling of your data is governed by Creem's privacy policy: https://www.creem.io/legal/privacy

## 3. Information you send us directly

If you email us at support@atelierartisanaldigital.com, we hold the contents of that correspondence and your email address so we can answer you and keep a record of the exchange.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
- We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.
- We do not add you to a marketing mailing list. Emails we send you relate to your purchase, your licence, or a support request you initiated.
- We do not use your data to train machine learning models.

## 6. Retention

We keep purchase records for as long as required by Canadian tax and accounting law, which is generally six years. We keep support correspondence for as long as it is useful for supporting you, and delete it on request where we are not required to retain it.

---

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate
- Ask us to delete your information, where we are not legally required to keep it
- Withdraw consent to our processing, where processing is based on consent
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email **support@atelierartisanaldigital.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

Residents of Quebec have rights under the *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector*, as amended by Law 25. Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR. Where the GDPR applies, our lawful bases for processing are performance of a contract (delivering your licence and support) and compliance with a legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

Person responsible for the protection of personal information: **Ugo Di Profio**, support@atelierartisanaldigital.com
